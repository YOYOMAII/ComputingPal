ComputingPal helps computing students revise with focused quizzes, understand answers through clear explanations, and return to saved practice results.

We’re building a more approachable way to learn computing: one that makes room for curiosity, hands-on practice, and steady progress.

## A better way to practise

- **Choose a topic** and focus your revision where you need it.
- **Try questions** that help you put computing ideas into practice.
- **Review explanations** to understand the reasoning behind an answer.
- **Return to your results** and keep building your understanding over time.

## Why we build

Computing can feel abstract or intimidating when learners lack a clear path and enough opportunities to practise. ComputingPal aims to make difficult ideas easier to approach and learning more practical for every student.

We believe that:

- Practice helps turn confusion into understanding.
- Mistakes are a useful part of learning.
- Everyone should have the opportunity to learn computing.

Read [Why We Build](https://computingpal.com/why-we-build) for the full story.
